Kilomi Support

Kilomi is a work mileage logbook for iPhone. Here is how to reach us, and answers to the most common questions.

Location permission

Kilomi uses location only to measure the distance of trips that you start. Nothing is recorded outside a trip, and the data never leaves your iPhone.

When iOS asks, choose “Allow While Using App”. If “Always” is ever needed to start trips from CarPlay while your iPhone is locked, the app will tell you.

You can see the permission’s status in Kilomi’s Settings → Status, and change it in iPhone Settings, under Kilomi. Without location permission you can still add trips by hand.

A trip won’t start

Open Kilomi’s Settings and look at the Status section: it shows whether location and Live Activities are in order and whether the widget’s Start button is ready.

Kilomi needs Live Activities to record: they are what lets the app keep measuring while your iPhone is locked. If they are off, the app does not start the trip and tells you why. Turn them on in the app’s settings (look for “Live Activities”).

If you have reached the month’s free limit, a new trip can’t be started either (see Free limit and one-time purchase).

Widget in CarPlay

Kilomi does not have its own CarPlay app: what you see in the car is its widget and the trip’s Live Activity.

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings → General → CarPlay and tap your car.
  2. Open Widgets and add the “Trip” widget from Kilomi.
  3. On the car’s screen, widgets are to the left of the Dashboard. Tap Start when you set off and End when you arrive.

Widgets in CarPlay need iOS 26 or later, and their buttons need a car with a touchscreen.

The same widget can go on your iPhone’s Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot, tap +, search for Kilomi and choose the small widget.

While a trip is running, the widget shows the elapsed time. iOS decides when a widget refreshes, so the distance it shows can lag behind; the up-to-the-moment distance is in the Live Activity (Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and CarPlay).

Automatic start with Shortcuts

It’s optional. With an automation in the Shortcuts app, the trip starts by itself when CarPlay connects and ends when it disconnects. If the widget button or the app’s button is enough for you, you don’t need this.

The step-by-step guide is inside the app, in Settings → Advanced options → Automatic start with Shortcuts. In short:

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and go to the Automation tab.
  2. Tap +, choose CarPlay, then the option for when it connects.
  3. Leave “Automation” on and “Notifications” off so it runs without asking you.
  4. Tap “Search”, type Kilomi and choose “Start Trip”.
  5. Tap the back arrow: the shortcut saves itself.
  6. Repeat the steps choosing the option for when it disconnects and the “End Trip” action.

If your car connects over Bluetooth and has no CarPlay, choose Bluetooth instead of CarPlay and select your car.

If your car connects to CarPlay normally this should work, but we can’t promise it in every car. Button names may differ slightly between iOS versions. With the iPhone locked, the “Always” location permission may be needed; the app will tell you.

Free limit and one-time purchase

Kilomi is free for up to 20 trips per month, with every feature. Every trip that starts in that month counts: work or personal, recorded or added by hand.

The full version is a one-time payment, with no subscription, and has no trip limit. You buy it inside the app, in Settings → Full version → See full version, and it is shared with Family Sharing. There are no accounts or sign-ups: the purchase goes with your Apple Account.

Even when you reach the limit, your saved trips can always be viewed, edited and exported.

Apple handles the payment. A refund is requested from Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com; Apple explains how in Request a refund for apps or content that you bought from Apple.

Restore the purchase

If you change iPhone or reinstall Kilomi, open Settings → Full version and tap “Restore Purchases” (it is also on the full-version screen). Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

If you see “No purchases found”, that Apple Account doesn’t have the full version: check which account your iPhone is signed in with.

Export your data

In Trips, go to the month you want, tap “More actions” and choose “Share report as PDF” or “Share report as CSV”. The report leaves your iPhone only when you share it, and goes to the destination you choose.

The report includes the month’s work trips: date, origin, destination, distance, purpose and amount. Personal trips are not included.

The PDF includes your details (name, company or tax ID, vehicle or plate) only if you turn on “Include my details in the report” in Settings → Report details. The CSV never includes them.

Delete your data

All the data is on your iPhone; we have none of it, so there is nothing to ask us to delete.

  • A trip: in the list, swipe it left and tap “Delete” (or touch and hold it and choose “Delete”). This can’t be undone.
  • A remembered place: in Settings → Remembered places, swipe it left and tap “Delete”. Deleting a place doesn’t change existing trips.
  • Everything: delete Kilomi from your iPhone. This removes the data stored on the iPhone; backups you made earlier and reports you already shared remain until you delete them.

Distances are approximate, measured by GPS. Review the data before submitting it: its accuracy is the responsibility of whoever submits it. This is not tax advice.
