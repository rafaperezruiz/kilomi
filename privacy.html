Kilomi privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Who is responsible

Rafael Pérez Ruiz (the "developer"). Privacy contact: rafa.perez.apps@gmail.com.

Summary

Kilomi is a mileage logbook for iPhone. The developer does not receive, store or process any personal data of the people who use the app. Everything you log stays on your iPhone. The app has no accounts, uses no servers of its own, contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party code, and makes no network connections of its own.

What data is stored on your iPhone

  • Trips: start and end date and time, distance, kind (work or personal), purpose or client, origin and destination names if you type them, the rate applied, and the coordinates (latitude and longitude) of the start and end points. The full route is not stored.
  • Remembered places: the names you give to an origin or destination (for example “Home” or “Office”) and their location, so they can be filled in for you next time.
  • Settings: distance unit, currency, rate, default trip kind and, if you enter them, your optional report details (name, company or tax ID, vehicle or plate).
  • The status of the full-version purchase.

This data exists only on your iPhone. The developer has no access to it.

Location

Kilomi uses the iPhone's location only while a trip that you started is being recorded (from the app, from the widget, or with a Shortcuts automation you created). While it records, iOS shows a Live Activity. Location is used to measure distance and is processed on the iPhone itself; it is not sent anywhere. You can change or withdraw the permission in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Kilomi. Without location permission you can still add trips by hand.

Reports

PDF and CSV reports are created by the app on your iPhone. They leave the iPhone only when you share them with the iOS share sheet, and they go to the destination you choose (mail, messages, files, etc.). From then on, whoever receives them handles them under their own terms. The PDF includes your personal details only if you turn that on in Settings. The CSV never includes them.

Purchases

The one-time purchase of the full version is made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and purchase data under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). The developer does not receive your payment details or your identity.

Backups

If your iPhone has backup turned on (iCloud or a computer), data from apps such as Kilomi may be part of that backup, which Apple or your computer manages and protects according to your settings. The developer has no access to those backups. Kilomi does not sync data with iCloud by itself.

Who data is shared with

No one. Kilomi does not sell or hand over data and uses no third-party services. There is no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete it. You can delete each trip inside the app, and deleting Kilomi removes the data stored on the iPhone (backups you made earlier and reports you already shared remain until you delete them).

Your rights

Because the developer does not process your personal data, there is no data about you for the developer to access, correct, erase or export. You control that data directly in the app (edit, delete, export). If you believe the developer processes data about you in any way, you can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) by contacting the developer through the contact details on Kilomi's App Store page. You can also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or with your local supervisory authority.

Children

Kilomi is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy (for example, by adding sync), this text will be updated first and the change announced on the App Store page and in the app.
